# Enable two-factor authentication

We strongly recommend enabling 2FA on your account, especially if it manages several services.

1. Log in to the [client area](https://clients.lklcloud.fr).
2. Open **Account** → **Security**.
3. Enable two-factor authentication and scan the QR code with an app like Google Authenticator, Authy or 1Password.
4. Keep the displayed backup codes somewhere safe — they let you recover access if you lose your phone.

Once enabled, you'll be asked for a 6-digit code at every login, in addition to your password.

## Signing in with Google

You can also sign in directly with your Google account, no separate password to remember, from the **Continue with Google** button on the login page.
