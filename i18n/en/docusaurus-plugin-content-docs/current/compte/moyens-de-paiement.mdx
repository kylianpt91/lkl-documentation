# Payment methods

## Credit card

Secure card payment, processed by Stripe. Your service is delivered automatically as soon as payment is confirmed.

## PayPal

Pay directly with your PayPal account from your invoice page.

## Bank transfer

On your invoice page, choose **Pay by bank transfer**. You'll get:

- LKL Cloud's IBAN and BIC
- The exact amount to transfer
- A **unique reference** that must be included in the transfer description

:::danger Reference required
Without this reference in the transfer description, your payment can't be matched automatically and your service won't be delivered right away. A manual match by our team is still possible but takes longer.
:::

Once you've made the transfer, click **I've made the transfer: check now** for an immediate check, instead of waiting for the automatic check (every 15 minutes).

- **Instant transfer**: invoice settled within minutes.
- **Standard transfer**: within 1–2 business days.
