# Getting started with your Linux VPS

After your order is paid, your VPS is deployed automatically (usually within a few minutes). You'll get an email as soon as it's ready.

## Finding your connection details

From the [client area](https://clients.lklcloud.fr), open **My services** → your VPS. You'll find:

- Your server's IP address
- The installed system (Debian or Ubuntu, based on your choice at checkout)
- The vCores, RAM and disk of your plan

## Connecting via SSH

If you added a public key in **Settings → SSH keys** *before* ordering, it's injected automatically:

```bash
ssh root@YOUR_IP
```

Otherwise, a root password is generated and shown once on the service page (blurred, click to reveal or copy). Change it on your first connection.

<div className="admonition admonition-note">
Adding an SSH key <em>after</em> the service was created does not add it to an already-active VPS automatically: open a ticket to have it added manually in the meantime.
</div>

## Rescue console

If SSH isn't responding, your service page offers a **console**: you type a command, confirm, and the result appears after a few seconds to a minute. This isn't an interactive terminal like SSH (no live file editing, one command at a time).

## Backups

There's no periodic automatic backup yet: each backup is triggered manually, from the **Backups** tab of the service page.

- **€1** charged on demand, automatically refunded if the backup fails for a technical reason.
- Ready within a few minutes.
- Free, one-click restore.

## Tracking usage

The **Usage** tab shows the real evolution of CPU, RAM and network over the last 30 days (measurements reported every minute, not simulated).

## Actions available from the client area

From your VPS's service page:

- **Start / Restart / Stop**
- **Change plan** (actually resizes vCPU/RAM/disk)
- **Clone** the service into a new instance
