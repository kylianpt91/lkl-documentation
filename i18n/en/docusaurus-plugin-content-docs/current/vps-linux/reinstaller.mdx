# Reinstalling or changing your system

Self-service OS reinstallation from the client area is **not yet available**.

If you need a clean system on your VPS, [open a ticket](https://clients.lklcloud.fr/support/nouveau) with:

- the service concerned,
- the distribution you want (Debian or Ubuntu).

Our team will take care of it manually.

:::warning All data will be erased
As with any reinstallation, the disk is fully replaced. If you have data to keep, make a [backup](/vps-linux/premiers-pas#backups) before opening the ticket.
:::
