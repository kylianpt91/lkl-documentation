# Frequently asked questions

### How long does it take to receive my service after payment?

Usually a few minutes. You'll get an email as soon as it's ready.

### Can I change my plan during my subscription?

Yes, at any time, from the relevant service page. The difference is prorated.

### How do I cancel automatic renewal?

From the client area, service page → turn off **Automatic renewal**. The service stays active until the end of the period already paid for.

### I paid but my service isn't showing up

- By card/PayPal: this is normally instant. Refresh the invoice page.
- By bank transfer: make sure you included the requested reference. Use the **Check now** button on the invoice page.

If the problem persists after a few minutes, contact support.

### How do I contact you?

From the client area, open a support ticket (fastest response), or by email at [support@lklcloud.fr](mailto:support@lklcloud.fr).
