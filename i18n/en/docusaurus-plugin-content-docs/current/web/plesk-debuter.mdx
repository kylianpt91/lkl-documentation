# Getting started with your web hosting

Your web hosting plan runs on the **Plesk** panel, accessible from your service page in the [client area](https://clients.lklcloud.fr) (**Open Plesk** button).

## Adding your site

1. In Plesk, open **Websites & Domains**.
2. Add your domain name (or use the temporary subdomain provided if your domain isn't active yet).
3. Upload your files via the built-in **file manager** or over FTP/SFTP (credentials under **FTP Accounts**).

## SSL certificate

A free **Let's Encrypt** certificate can be enabled in one click from your domain's **SSL/TLS** tab — renewal is automatic.

## Databases

**Databases** → **Add a database**. The number of available databases depends on your plan (see the summary on your service page).

## Backups

Automatic daily backups are kept for 7 days (30 days with the "Extended backups" option). Restore directly from Plesk, **Backup/Restore** tab.
