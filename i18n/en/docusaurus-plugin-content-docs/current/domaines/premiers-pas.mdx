# Managing your domain name

After registration, your domain appears in the [client area](https://clients.lklcloud.fr), under **Domains**. Registration usually takes effect within a few minutes.

## Nameservers

By default, your domain uses LKL Cloud's DNS servers (`dns1.lklcloud.fr`, `dns2.lklcloud.fr`) — this is what lets you manage your records directly from the client area. You can replace them with third-party nameservers from the **Nameservers** tab of the domain page — in that case, the DNS zone must be managed with that third party instead of LKL Cloud.

## DNS records

From the **DNS** tab of the domain page, add or edit your records (A, AAAA, CNAME, MX, TXT...). A change usually takes a few minutes to a few hours to propagate, depending on the chosen TTL.

Example to point your domain to an LKL Cloud VPS:

| Type | Name | Value |
|---|---|---|
| A | @ | Your VPS's IP |
| CNAME | www | yourdomain.com |

## Redirects

The **Redirects** tab lets you redirect the domain (or a subdomain) to another address, or redirect emails to another inbox, without manual DNS configuration.

## Automatic renewal

Enabled by default. You can disable it from the domain page — in that case, remember to renew manually before expiration (`expiresAt` shown on the page), after which the domain becomes publicly available again after a short grace period.

## Security

- **Transfer lock**: prevents a transfer of the domain to another registrar without your explicit approval. Enabled by default — only disable it temporarily if you're initiating a transfer yourself.
- **WHOIS privacy**: hides your personal details from the public WHOIS directory.
- **DNSSEC**: cryptographic signing of your DNS zone against certain spoofing attacks. Enable it from the domain page if your domain supports it.
