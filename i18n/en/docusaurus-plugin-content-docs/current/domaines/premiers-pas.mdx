# Managing your domain name

:::info Coming soon
Domain name registration is not yet open for self-service ordering. This page describes what will be available once it launches.
:::

## Nameservers

By default, a domain will use LKL Cloud's DNS servers (`dns1.lklcloud.fr`, `dns2.lklcloud.fr`). You'll be able to replace them with third-party nameservers from the **Nameservers** tab of the domain page.

## DNS zone

The **DNS** tab of the domain page lets you view your records (A, AAAA, CNAME, MX, TXT...). Editing them in self-service isn't available yet: for a change, [open a ticket](https://clients.lklcloud.fr/support/nouveau) with the domain and the records you need, and our team will apply it for you.

## Automatic renewal

Can be enabled from the domain page.
