# Deploying your Discord bot

:::info Coming soon
Discord bot hosting (Node.js and Python) is not yet open for self-service ordering. This page describes what will be available once it launches.
:::

Your bots will be managed through the **Pterodactyl** panel, accessible from your service page in the [client area](https://clients.lklcloud.fr): uploading your files (or cloning a Git repository), environment variables for your Discord token, starting the bot with live logs in the console, and automatic restart if it crashes.

## Need a bot before launch?

Write to us at [support@lklcloud.fr](mailto:support@lklcloud.fr) and we'll get back to you once this service launches.
