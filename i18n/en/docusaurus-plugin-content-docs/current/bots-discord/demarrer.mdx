# Deploying your Discord bot

Your bots (Node.js or Python) are managed through the **Pterodactyl** panel, accessible from your service page in the [client area](https://clients.lklcloud.fr).

## Uploading your code

1. Open the panel, **Files** tab.
2. Upload your files (or clone a Git repository from the dedicated tab).
3. Set your **Discord token** as an environment variable (never hardcoded in your code).

## Starting the bot

- **Node.js**: the default start command is `npm start` (editable under **Startup**).
- **Python**: `python bot.py` by default.

Click **Start** at the top of the panel. Logs are shown live in the console.

## Does the bot restart on its own?

Yes: if it crashes, the service restarts automatically. You can also schedule regular restarts from the **Task scheduling** tab.

## Scaling up resources

If your bot grows, change your plan directly from the client area (service page → **Change plan**) — no reinstall needed, your files are kept.
